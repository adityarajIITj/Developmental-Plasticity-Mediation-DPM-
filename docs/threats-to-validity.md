# Threats to validity

## Construct validity

A measured change in learning rate or evidence sensitivity may not uniquely represent learned significance or functional affect. Operational measures need a clear rationale and alternative interpretations.

## Internal validity

A mediator intervention may alter variables other than the intended learning pathway. Pair initial states and evidence sequences where appropriate, and test intervention isolation.

## Comparative validity

A weak or under-tuned generic meta-learning baseline can make a specialized model look distinctive. Match information, data exposure, compute, and tuning budgets and disclose unavoidable differences.

## External validity

A result on a small set of tasks may not generalize to other environments or forms of learning. Define the target domain and use held-out contexts.

## Statistical validity

Single-seed outcomes can be unstable. Specify the aggregation, uncertainty reporting, exclusions, and analysis plan before evaluation.

## Interpretive validity

Successful functional predictions do not establish subjective feeling or phenomenal valence. Keep behavioral, causal-organizational, and phenomenal claims separate.
