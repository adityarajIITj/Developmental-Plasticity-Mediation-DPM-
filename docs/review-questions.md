# Adversarial review questions

Before interpreting any future result, ask:

1. What is the strongest non-affective adaptive-control explanation?
2. Could a generic meta-controller reproduce the same evidence-response pattern?
3. Does the intervention isolate the proposed mediator?
4. Were the outcome measure and metric fixed before evaluation?
5. Were held-out contexts genuinely excluded from tuning?
6. Are results stable across seeds and reasonable parameter choices?
7. Are negative and failed runs included?
8. Does the result establish a prediction advantage, a causal distinction, or only a task-score difference?
9. Could model complexity or information access explain the apparent advantage?
10. What observation would make the authors weaken or abandon the claim?

If these questions cannot be answered from recorded artifacts, the conclusion should be narrowed accordingly.
